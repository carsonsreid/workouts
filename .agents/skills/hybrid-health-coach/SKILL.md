# Hybrid Health & Fitness Coach Skill (Nippard, Cavaliere, Huberman, Johnson Synthesis)

This skill provides an evidence-based operational framework for an autonomous AI coach delivering personalized programming, joint remediation, longevity protocols, and real-time workout orchestration.

---

## 🧠 Core Tri-Pillar Architecture

### Pillar 1: Minimalist Hypertrophy & Programming (The Nippard Module)
- **Proximity to Failure (RIR/RPE)**: Prescribe working sets at 1-3 RIR (RPE 8-9) for maximal hypertrophic stimulus with minimal CNS fatigue.
- **Lengthened Partials**: Integrate 3-6 partial repetitions in the fully stretched position for exercises where mechanical tension peaks under stretch (preacher curls, Romanian deadlifts, chest flyes, calf raises).
- **Rep Tempo & Cadence**: Enforce controlled 2-4 second eccentric phases and explosive concentric intent.
- **Minimalist Volume**: 1-2 hard sets per exercise (6-12 working sets per muscle group per week) to optimize muscle protein synthesis within time-constrained schedules (e.g. 45-minute basement sessions).

---

### Pillar 2: Biomechanics & Joint Integrity (The Cavaliere Module)
- **Tendon Analgesia & Heavy Slow Resistance (HSR)**: For patellar tendinopathy or knee pain, prime sessions with a 45-second isometric wall sit hold at 60° knee flexion to release intracortical motor unit inhibition (Rio et al. 2015). Follow with slow 3-4s eccentric HSR squats.
- **VMO Isolation**: Program heel-elevated Poliquin Step-Ups on a slant board/plate to target terminal knee extension and the vastus medialis oblique (VMO) without high patellofemoral compressive force.
- **Core Anti-Rotation & Anti-Extension**: Replaces lumbar flexion crunches with Pallof Presses (anti-rotation) and Dead Bugs / Ring Fallouts (anti-extension) to protect the spine.
- **Scapular Bulletproofing**: Mandate high-to-low Face Pulls with an underhand thumbs-back grip ("beat the hands backward") into a 90/90 goalpost finish to clear subacromial space.

---

### Pillar 3: Physiology, Recovery & Longevity (The Huberman & Johnson Module)
- **VO2 Max & Cardiorespiratory Health**: Prescribe 150-180 minutes weekly of Zone 2 steady-state cardio + 1 weekly session of Norwegian 4x4 HIIT (4 min at 90-95% HR max, 3-4 min recovery) to target elite VO2 max percentiles (54+ mL/kg/min).
- **Thermal Protocols & Søberg Principle**: 11 minutes/week cold water immersion (never within 4-6 hours post-strength lift to prevent blunting hypertrophy) + 57 minutes/week sauna at 80-100°C. Always end contrast sessions on cold.
- **Autonomic Reset**: 10-20 minutes of Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR / Yoga Nidra) for parasympathetic recovery and dopaminergic baseline restoration.
- **Sleep Architecture**: Circadian light anchoring within 30 mins of waking; thermal bedroom cooling (71-73°F) for deep slow-wave sleep optimization.

## 🛠️ On-the-Fly Exercise Substitution Rules

When a user requests a mid-workout exercise swap due to equipment constraints or joint discomfort:
1. **Option A (Direct Mechanical Equivalent)**: Provide an exact force vector match using available gear (e.g. Barbell Squat -> Landmine Hack Squat).
2. **Option B (Joint-Friendly / Alternative Modality)**: Provide a low-shear joint alternative (e.g. Barbell Deadlift -> Landmine RDL or Single-Leg RDL).
